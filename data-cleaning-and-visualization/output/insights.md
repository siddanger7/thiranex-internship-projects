# Sales Analytics - Key Insights

- **Dataset**: 980 cleaned transactions, 5 regions x 5 categories.
- **Total revenue**: $199,267.70
- **Best region**: North ($59,575.62)
- **Best category**: Clothing ($57,937.47)
- **Best product**: Headphones ($25,857.12)
- **Average order value**: $203.33
- **Busiest periods**: monthly peak revenue trend shown in `04_monthly_trend.png`.
- Price outliers (IQR method) were removed; see price distribution chart before/after.
- `price` and `quantity` correlate strongly with `revenue` (by construction).
